# Project 0: Is a hot dog a sandwich?

Investigating our hypothesis that 1/4 of UVA full-time undergraduate students believe a hot dog is a sandwich.

Collected the data by having UVA students fill out a survey. The results are in Hotdog Survey (Responses).xlsx, and the data dictionary is data_dictionary.md.
